Vastu Floor Plan Tool

How to use

Trace a hand-drawn sketch
  1. Click "Upload Sketch" and choose a photo of your hand-drawn plan.
  2. Lower the opacity slider so it shows faintly.
  3. Use "Plot Boundary" to trace the outer wall first.
  4. Use "Wall" and "Room" to trace rooms on top.
  5. Hide or remove the sketch once traced.
Irregular plots & sizes
  1. Most plots aren't perfect rectangles. Select the boundary, then drag any corner dot independently — each wall keeps its own length.
  2. Double-tap along a boundary wall to add a new corner (for L-shaped plots). Double-tap a corner to remove it.
  3. Every wall, room edge and boundary edge shows its length live.
  4. Set "1 grid square = __ ft" in Scale to match your real plot.
