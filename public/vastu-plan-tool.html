How to use

Trace a hand-drawn sketch
  1. Click "Upload Sketch" and choose a photo of your hand-drawn plan.
  2. Lower the opacity slider so it shows faintly.
  3. Use "Plot Boundary" to trace the outer wall first.
  4. Use "Wall" and "Room" to trace rooms on top.
Zoom, irregular shapes & sizes
  1. Pinch (mobile) or scroll (desktop) on the canvas to zoom. Drag empty space to pan.
  2. Zoom out + keep Snap on to place walls quickly in whole feet. Zoom in + turn Snap off to fine-tune inches.
  3. Select the boundary or any room, then drag any corner dot independently — each wall keeps its own length.
  4. Double-tap along a wall to add a new corner (L-shapes), double-tap a corner to remove it.
  5. Select a door/window and drag its blue dot to resize or reorient it.
